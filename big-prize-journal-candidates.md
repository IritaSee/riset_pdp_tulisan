# Big-Prize Journal Candidates — CiPA Solver Fidelity & Hardware Portability Study

**Purpose:** Scopus-accredited targets beyond the quick-turnaround tier (IJECE / BEE&I / JOIV), aimed at internationalizing this paper. All figures below were pulled from journal metric aggregators in August 2026 — **verify current APC, quartile, and turnaround directly on the publisher's site before submitting**, as these move often and some sources disagreed with each other.

---

## 1. International Journal for Numerical Methods in Biomedical Engineering (IJNMBE)

- **Publisher:** Wiley
- **Indexing:** Scopus, SCIE
- **Quartile / IF:** Q2 (Applied Mathematics, Computational Theory & Mathematics, Modeling & Simulation); IF ≈ 2.1–2.6 (sources varied slightly)
- **Scope fit:** ★★★★★ — Explicitly covers differential-equation-based biomedical models and both established and novel numerical solution methods (finite difference/element/volume, etc.). This is close to a direct match for a solver-comparison paper.
- **Format note:** Runs a two-track structure — "Part A: Fundamentals" for new numerical methods/solutions, and an applied track. Our paper likely fits Fundamentals.
- **Cost:** Hybrid journal — subscription route may have no APC; gold OA fee not confirmed, needs checking.
- **Verdict:** Strongest single topical match. Top recommendation if choosing one target.

---

## 2. Cardiovascular Engineering and Technology (CVET)

- **Publisher:** Springer, affiliated with BMES (Biomedical Engineering Society)
- **Indexing:** Scopus, SCIE, PubMed, Embase
- **Quartile / IF:** Q2–Q3 (Biomedical Engineering; Cardiology & Cardiovascular Medicine); IF ≈ 1.8–2.4
- **Scope fit:** ★★★★☆ — Cardiac-specific, explicitly welcomes electrophysiology and computational/translational cardiovascular research. Strong disciplinary and society-imprint fit.
- **Cost:** APC ≈ **$3,590 USD** for gold OA — steep for a "small push." Check whether Telkom University or the KIP graduate support program has a waiver/institutional deal before committing.
- **Verdict:** Best pick if the cardiology-specific audience and BMES imprint matter more than saving on APC.

---

## 3. Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine — Update

- **Publisher:** Elsevier
- **Indexing:** Scopus
- **Notes:** Newer, less selective sister journal to the Q1 flagship *Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine* (already on the stretch list). Carries the CMPB name recognition with a more forgiving novelty bar.
- **Scope fit:** ★★★★☆ — Good match for the reproducibility / structured-logging framing of the paper specifically.
- **Verdict:** Solid middle-ground safety net between the IAES quick tier and the two picks above.

---

## Honorable mention: MethodsX

- **Publisher:** Elsevier
- **Indexing:** Scopus, ESCI, DOAJ, PubMed, Embase
- **APC:** Reported figures ranged **$530–$1,380 USD** across sources — confirm current price.
- **Turnaround:** ~7 weeks average (fast)
- **Format note:** Structured around short "customized method" write-ups, often as a **companion piece** to a main research article rather than a full IMRaD study.
- **Suggested use:** Not a fit for the main manuscript, but a good candidate for a **satellite paper** describing the logging schema / benchmarking protocol itself, published alongside wherever the main study lands.
